# Changelog

## 1.3.2

- **Fixed: DOTS uneven when another mod resizes the picture** (Kanto Gear's layout on a phone, among others): the dot lines were drawn into the picture at the game's own size, so a mod drawing it a little bigger or smaller doubled some lines and lost others, more on one side than the other. Facets now sees the picture drawn at a size of its own and draws the lines over it there, one on each game pixel as shown. DOTS also follows a GBA game stretched more one way than the other.
- **DOTS with WoobleView** (gen3-hd-sprites by poooooby): with Fill Screen, a battle's scene is zoomed and its Pokémon drawn at the screen's own resolution, so there are no game pixels there for the dots, and they beat against the menus WoobleView draws a little bigger. DOTS now follows WoobleView's own placing of the text box and menus, one line on each of their pixels, evenly across the whole screen.

## 1.3.1

- **GAME WOOD** (WINDOWS and TEXT BOX, on every game): Tito Seich / Sage's Woody box in a game's own colour: RED, BLUE, YELLOW, GOLD, SILVER, CRYSTAL, EMERALD and LEAFGREEN WOOD, each pale, and DARK in the game's rich colour with white or black letters, whichever reads better.
- **PAPER to match GAME WOOD:** each game's pale colour and its DARK one as a PAPER too. DARK RED, BLUE, SILVER, CRYSTAL and EMERALD are dark papers with white letters; DARK YELLOW, GOLD and LEAFGREEN are strong light ones with black letters (the GBA games' own grey letters inked black on them), kept to a battle's boxes as the dark papers are, and pictures keep their white on them as on dark paper (the summary, the POKéDEX). On FireRed to Emerald they're only in the windows, as dark paper is, so the game's own screens (the party, the POKéDEX) keep their white and their white letters; the title menu's blue letters go navy, without the white behind them.
- **Choices for games you haven't imported are skipped** in FACETS: without Ruby or Sapphire there are no R/S frames to step through, without Emerald no EMERALD frames, and the same for the other games' frames, scenes and looks. Their ABOUT page still lists them, with the game they need.
- **Fixed: PAPER crashing on entering a battle, and slowing the game before it**, on phones and other small devices. Each time a frame drew on something of a different size (Gold, Silver and Crystal's battles draw on two), PAPER made a new full-size copy for what it keeps, dozens a second; small devices ran out of memory. The copies are made once for each size now and kept.
- **Fixed: Kanto Gear's hidden battle text box** (FireRed to Emerald, BATTLE BG: SCENERY): Kanto Gear 4.6.5 paints the game's own ground into the text box's place when it hides it, which landed over Facets' scene as a pale strip. The scene is drawn straight by Facets now, its ground stretched down into that space (BORDERS: FILL's big scene covers it already).
- **PAPER DETAIL** (MENUS, under PAPER), for small devices: **POTATO** has dark paper turn letters white without their outline, looking only at each letter's four next pixels (a black touching the paper is a letter; the wipe into a battle, which touches none, stays black), which saves most of dark paper's work on a handheld's graphics chip. **FULL** (the default) is as before.
- **PRECACHE** (MENUS, under PAPER DETAIL): makes PAPER's windows and letters for your settings ahead, a few milliseconds a frame, and keeps them on your install, so menus and battles don't stall the first time they come up. Again for a new PAPER, WINDOWS or TEXT BOX. OFF by default.
- **Faster PAPER on dark paper:** what PAPER keeps as it is each frame (letters, pictures, a frame's own white, the boxes it darkens) was drawn onto its copy one at a time, going to the copy and back over a hundred times a frame; on a handheld's graphics chip each of those is slow. It's written down as it's drawn now and put on the copy in one go before the pass. A dark-paper battle costs well under two-thirds what it did.
- **PAPER's pictures are kept on your install:** the window art and letters PAPER makes for its colour are written to the engine's per-install mod cache (the same place PREBUILD keeps its pictures, whether PREBUILD is on or not), so a small device makes each one once, as it first comes up, and reads it back on later starts. Picking another PAPER clears them to be made again, and so does a new Facets or gen1recomp.
- **ANIMATION: LOOP on Gold, Silver and Crystal** loops Crystal's animations too (Crystal's own, and the CRYSTAL and EMERALD looks on Gold and Silver): while the battle waits for your command, each Pokémon that's out plays its animation again after a rest, with no cry, yours too when YOUR SIDE shows its front. Before, they played once as they came out.
- **Fixed: SURROUND: WORLD in stripes on phones and handhelds** (Gold, Silver and Crystal): on a screen with a DPI scale over 1 the kept picture of the map was laid back shrunk into the top left, its edge smeared into stripes over the rest of the border. It covers the window as it should now, with the voxel diorama too.
- **Fixed: letters flashing and sprites patched dark with PAPER** (FACETS's own pages on Emerald, among others): making that new copy threw away what the first had kept, every other frame.

- Tools: `ips_extract.py` reads `.ups` patches as well as `.ips`. ## 1.0.0

Facets now covers all eleven of gen1recomp's games: Red, Blue, Yellow, Gold, Silver and Crystal join FireRed, LeafGreen, Ruby, Sapphire and Emerald. Facets, Emerald Lens and Crystal Lens all move to 1.0.0 together. Needs gen1recomp 0.3.98 or newer.

### The Game Boy games

- **A FACETS page** on the START and OPTION menus, shared with Emerald Lens and Crystal Lens through **PRISM**. Settings are saved with the game's options, and the last row, **CREDITS**, names everyone whose art Facets shows.
- **NPCS:** COMMUNITY, DIEGO, TAIGA and ZAFFRE on the Game Boy games, walking on both of Gen 3's steps, with changes showing straight away.
- **Fill-in sets** so every person has art: **JOHTO DS** (Jext, KyleDove and figyberries) for Johto's leaders, Elite Four and story people and Kanto's workers and staff, **DIONYUS1** for Yellow's Jessie, James and Officer Jenny, and the Gen 3-style **HEART & SOUL** and **KALARIE** for the GBA look. A person's own name is looked for in every set before any stand-in, so Mom in Crystal is Johto's and a Lass is a Lass.
- **PIKACHU** (Yellow): seven looks by vitorluiscolnago and piphybuilder88, with leParagon's battle pictures, worn by your partner Pikachu on the map, in battle, on its summary and in the Hall of Fame.
- **TRAINERS: DS** gives every trainer class a picture, Johto's included, with Young-Dante's Teacher and L-mon's Jessie and James.
- **POKEMON: DS** and **YOUR SIDE**, plus **CRYSTAL** (Crystal's own sprites) on Red, Blue and Yellow.
- **ICONS:** **MINIDEX**, a different icon for every Pokémon, and **CRYSTAL** on Red, Blue and Yellow.
- **The GBA look:** FIRERED and EMERALD under NPCS, TRAINERS and POKEMON, read from your own imported games, with Heart & Soul's and kalarie's Gen 3-style people for anyone neither game has, small walkers (the Little Boy and Little Girl) included. Emerald's Pokémon animate on Gold, Silver and Crystal.
- **SIZE** for Pokémon and for trainers, each on its own: FIT, FULL, SMALL or TINY.
- **BATTLE BG: SCENERY** behind the battle, picked from where you are (in wide battles on Red, Blue and Yellow), and **BATTLE UI** sets in wide battles.
- **WINDOWS:** FireRed's ten frames and its text box, or a TITO style, for every box in the game; the FACETS page shows the style on its own row.
- **COLORS** keeps the game in its full-colour mode while anything in Facets is on, and gives your own choice back when it's off.
- **The GB filter**, shared with the Gen 3 games, with three new colours: **YELLOW**, **GOLD** and **CRYSTAL** (in every game).
- **Achievements** count on the Game Boy games, with six of their own: **Advanced Class**, **Crystal Clear**, **Pika Pika!**, **Window Shopping**, **The Big Picture** and **Roll the Credits**. Each achievement only shows in the games it can be earned in.

### The Gen 3 games

- The Mt. Chimney cable car no longer errors on sets' Campers and Picnickers: a sheet with more than the scene's 15 colours is cut down to 15 there.
- The battle box sets that shipped in 0.7.0 (DP, PLATINUM, HGSS, BW and the B2W2 sets) are in the repo.
- Fixes from Emerald Lens (thanks Shane McGovern): meta.json files with a UTF-8 BOM or `\u` escapes read correctly; red info lines on the FACETS screen take the room first; long words in CREDITS are cut to the window; a game imported mid-session shows up without a restart; Scenic Route and Gem Collector progress reloads with a save.

- `tools/import_rpgmaker.py` turns an RPG Maker sheet or a Gen 3 strip into a Game Boy overworld sheet.
- `tools/import_hns.py` copies Pokémon Heart & Soul's Johto people.
- `tools/make_pikachu.py` builds Yellow's Pikachu looks.
- `tools/make_gba_ids.py` writes the FireRed and Emerald sprite ids the GBA look reads.
